# Homepage (index.html)

Scope: public homepage / workshop list. Mode: Persuade (visitor decides on an event and registers).
Audience: first-time journalers from Instagram/WhatsApp on phones; returning warga. Action: tap Daftar on an open event card. Proof: real community photo, real reels, real event data (date, venue, seats). Constraints: event logic, honest data, WhatsApp hand-off, Indonesian casual voice, seminggu-ui fonts (Outfit + Open Sans) and blue/yellow brand stay.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The homepage is a 2000s school binder decorated by the community: every event is a loose-leaf sheet you can flip to. Refuses the link-in-bio column of identical cards.
OWN-WORLD: Brand-blue binder board with metal rings; white loose-leaf with pale-blue rules, pink margin and punched holes; yellow index tabs; kiss-cut stickers with white die-cut borders; translucent washi strips; Mochi as a sticker. Depth: paper, photo, sticker.
STORY: Visitors see a decorated binder (community, craft, beginner-safe), flip to the nearest event sheet, read date/venue/kit, and tap the Daftar sticker; warga find their way back in.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue binder cover filling the width: name on a white sticker label, Mochi and paw stickers, taped polaroid of real warga; the yellow "Jadwal Workshop" tab and the top of the first event sheet peek at the bottom edge.
FORM: Binder Diary 2000-an, position 5 of 7 on my list; seed:04faf994bebfbfb4. Signature interaction: the Daftar sticker peels at the corner on hover/press. Motion: stickers stick once on load (scale-settle), nothing loops.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
