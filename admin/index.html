Leads & Outreach

Track prospects from Instagram, TikTok, & Threads. So you don't lose track of who's been chatted, who's promising, and who's worth unfollowing.

Import Instagram Data

How to get the data (click to open the guide)
  1. Open Instagram → Profile → menu ☰ → Accounts Center (or: Settings → Meta Accounts Center).
  2. Choose Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Click Download or transfer information → select your IG account.
  4. Choose Some of your information → scroll to the Connections section → check Followers and following.
  5. Click Next. Important: choose Download to device, Date range: All time, and Format: JSON (not HTML!).
  6. Click Create files. Wait for the email from Instagram (usually a few minutes), then download the ZIP file & extract it.
  7. Inside the connections/followers_and_following/ folder there's following.json and one or more followers files: followers_1.json, followers_2.json, etc.
  8. IMPORTANT: select ALL those files at once — including any followers_2/3/... if present. If you only pick followers_1.json, older followers won't be read & will be wrongly flagged as "not following back".

Files are only processed in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
